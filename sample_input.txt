==============================================================================
  MCP-Powered Multi-Agent Air Quality Reasoning System — Sample Prompts
==============================================================================

Below are example prompts you can use with the MCP agent to invoke
different air-quality prediction tools. Copy-paste any prompt as-is
or modify the values to match your scenario.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. AQI FORECAST (Next 4 Hours)
   Tool: predict_aqi_forecast
   Requires 12 pollutant readings.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Predict the air quality index for the next 4 hours given these pollutant levels:
{
  "pm25": 85.4,
  "pm10": 110.2,
  "no": 15.1,
  "no2": 42.8,
  "nox": 55.3,
  "nh3": 8.4,
  "co": 1.2,
  "so2": 10.5,
  "o3": 62.1,
  "benzene": 2.1,
  "toluene": 5.4,
  "xylene": 1.8
}

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HEALTH RISK ESTIMATION
   Tool: estimate_health_risk
   Requires AQI + 8 environmental features.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Estimate the health risk for the following environmental conditions:
AQI = 128, PM2.5 = 85.4, PM10 = 110.2, NO2 = 42.8, SO2 = 10.5,
O3 = 62.1, Temperature = 32°C, Humidity = 65%, Wind Speed = 8 km/h.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VEHICLE EMISSION PREDICTION
   Tool: predict_vehicle_emission
   Requires engine size, mileage, and speed (+ optional parameters).
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Predict the emission level for a car with:
Engine Size = 2.0L, Mileage = 45000 km, Speed = 80 km/h,
Fuel Type = Gasoline, Vehicle Age = 5 years, Road Type = Highway.

Another example (diesel truck in city traffic):
Predict vehicle emissions for a truck with a 5.0L diesel engine,
120000 km mileage, driving at 30 km/h in city traffic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AIR POLLUTION IMAGE CLASSIFICATION
   Tool: classify_pollution_image
   Requires a path to an urban/outdoor image.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Classify the air pollution level from this image: C:\path\to\your\image.jpg

Analyze the air quality visible in the photo at: ./test_photos/city_view.png

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CHAINED / MULTI-TOOL PROMPTS
   The agent can combine multiple tools in a single query.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Given these pollutant levels — PM2.5: 120, PM10: 180, NO2: 55, SO2: 18,
O3: 45, CO: 2.1, NO: 22, NOx: 70, NH3: 12, Benzene: 3.5, Toluene: 8.2,
Xylene: 2.6 — first predict the AQI forecast for the next 4 hours, then
estimate the health risk assuming Temperature = 35°C, Humidity = 70%,
and Wind Speed = 5 km/h.

Also predict emissions for a 1.6L gasoline car driving at 60 km/h
with 30000 km mileage in moderate city traffic.
